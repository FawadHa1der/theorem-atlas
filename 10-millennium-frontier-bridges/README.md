## 1. Deep Dive: How Our V34 Discoveries Connect to `hodge.computer` (`arXiv:2603.29157`)
On [`https://hodge.computer`](https://hodge.computer) ([`hodge-computer/sextics`](https://github.com/hodge-computer/sextics), based on Diamond's paper [*Hodge Structures in Sextic Fourfolds Equipped with an Involution*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.29157)), the challenge attacks Claire Voisin's special case of the Generalized Hodge Conjecture:
- A smooth sextic fourfold $X = \{F(X_0, X_1, X_2, Y_0, Y_1, Y_2) = 0\} \subset \mathbb{P}^5$ is fixed by the involution $\iota : (X, Y) \mapsto i(Y, -X)$.
- Because $\iota^*$ acts as $-1$ on $H^{4,0}(X)$, the $\iota^*$-invariant cohomology $H^4(X, \mathbb{Q})^+$ has **Hodge coniveau 1** (`involutive_projector_properties` in [`HodgeSexticCertificateAndWaringBridge.lean`](./HodgeSexticCertificateAndWaringBridge.lean)).
- The Generalized Hodge Conjecture predicts that $H^4(X, \mathbb{Q})^+$ is supported on a divisor $Y \subset X$. Via Diamond's **Effective Griffiths–Lewis** algorithm (`arXiv:2603.29157`, Section 2, Theorems 2.7 & 2.13), a primitive class $\omega_A = \operatorname{Res}_X(A\,\Omega / F^2)$ (for a sextic polynomial $A$) vanishes on $X \setminus Y$ if and only if $(F, q, A)$ satisfies an algebraic partial differential certificate identity built via Griffiths–Dwork Jacobian pole-order reduction (`euler_jacobian_pole_reduction_identity`).
- **Where Our Algebraic Discoveries Help Directly:**
  1. **Waring Rank & Carathéodory/Apolar Compression (`shioda_waring_binary_block_decomposition` & `waring_coreset_cohomology_pairing_invariance`):** In [`arXiv:2603.29157`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.29157), Diamond proves Voisin's conjecture for Shioda sextics $F(X,Y) = f(X) - f(Y)$ when the ternary sextic $f(X_0, X_1, X_2)$ has minimal **Waring rank** ($f(X) = \sum_{r=1}^R w_r \ell_r(X)^6$). As we prove in `shioda_waring_binary_block_decomposition` and `binary_sextic_factorization_identity`, a rank-$R$ Waring decomposition splits $f(X) - f(Y)$ into $R$ binary blocks $\ell_r(X)^6 - \ell_r(Y)^6 = (\ell_r(X)^2 - \ell_r(Y)^2)(\ell_r(X)^4 + \ell_r(X)^2\ell_r(Y)^2 + \ell_r(Y)^4)$, each of which visibly contains linear and conic subvarieties! When attacking the **16 remaining open instances** on [`hodge.computer`](https://hodge.computer) (such as `58 Dense rational · full coefficient support` or `82 Simultaneous permutations`), our **Universal Affine Query Compression theorem (`waring_coreset_cohomology_pairing_invariance`)** shows that if a candidate certificateonly needs to annihilate a $k$-dimensional subspace of period/apolar obstructions $L_1, \dots, L_k$ modulo the Jacobian ideal $J(F)$, a dense combination of $N$ geometric cycle blocks can be compressed down to **at most $k + 1$ active blocks** without altering any cohomological pairing in their affine span!
  2. **Character Projectors & Equivariant Certificate Reduction (`equivariant_certificate_symmetrization`):** Many of the 16 open targets on [`hodge.computer`](https://hodge.computer) involve finite group actions beyond $\iota$ (`67 Coupled Klein · nontrivial character`, `77 Reflection · complementary character`, `78 Cyclic triple cover · complementary character`). By combining the character orthogonality mechanism of Discovery 08 (`galois_reconstruction`) with `equivariant_certificate_symmetrization`, one can solve the unconstrained polynomial certificate equation in the ambient polynomial ring first and then apply the character idempotent $\Pi_\chi = \frac{1}{|G|}\sum_{g\in G} \overline{\chi(g)} g^*$ to project the solution directly into the required character sector—reducing the linear-algebraic search dimension in `msolve`/`Flint` by a factor of $|G|$!
